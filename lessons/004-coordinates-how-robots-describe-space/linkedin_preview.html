A robot cannot act on “move over there” until “there” has a precise meaning.

This Professor OS lesson introduces coordinate maps through RoboRover and builds from simple ordered pairs to practical robotics reasoning:

• how origins, axes, signs, and scale define a map;
• how to calculate straight-line distance between locations;
• why signed coordinate differences preserve direction;
• how Python and matplotlib turn the mathematics into a visual experiment;
• why world-frame and body-frame coordinates are not interchangeable.

The lesson also makes an important engineering distinction: Euclidean distance is not always the distance a robot can drive around obstacles. Students test predictions, inspect plotted geometry, and use assertions to verify their calculations.

Try the lab with new rover and target coordinates. Predict first, calculate second, and run the program third.
