How can a robot predict where it will be without calculating every force and motor torque?

This Professor OS lesson introduces robot kinematics through RoboRover and connects equations directly to a runnable Python simulation.

Students learn to:
• Represent a planar pose with position and orientation.
• Convert forward body-frame velocity into world-frame motion.
• Predict circular-arc motion from speed and angular velocity.
• Compare approximate time-step updates with an exact constant-curvature update.
• Explain why wheel slip, calibration, latency, and mechanical limits separate a model from a real robot.

The prediction experiments are especially valuable: learners change speed and turn rate, state what should happen, then use the plot and equations to test their reasoning.

Try the lab with a simulated rover, then ask students how the result would change on a real floor with unequal wheels or slipping tires.
