A robot can estimate its position without GPS, cameras, or a map—but that estimate is never automatically perfect.

This Professor OS lesson introduces odometry through RoboRover’s differential-drive motion:

• Convert left- and right-wheel travel into forward displacement and heading change.
• Use midpoint integration to update a robot pose.
• See why wheel slip, calibration errors, and track-width mistakes create drift.
• Compare a modeled square path with an odometry estimate in Python.
• Test accumulated error through a safe tabletop dead-reckoning activity.

The central engineering lesson is simple: odometry is excellent for estimating short-term motion, but uncertainty grows as measurements accumulate. That makes calibration and sensor fusion essential in practical mobile robots.

Try the lab by changing the distance scale, turn error, or starting heading, then observe how the estimated route separates from the modeled reference.
