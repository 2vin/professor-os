A segmented image tells a robot which pixels may belong to an object. Contours help answer the next engineering question: what structure does that region have?

In this Professor OS lesson, students learn to:
- distinguish a filled region from its boundary;
- measure area, perimeter, bounding boxes, and centroids;
- simplify contours into polygons and reason about corners;
- understand holes, touching objects, noise, and retrieval modes;
- build and test a complete OpenCV contour pipeline with synthetic data.

The lesson also emphasizes an essential robotics habit: pixel measurements are evidence, not physical truth. A robust robot combines shape with color, position, segmentation quality, and frame-to-frame consistency.

Try the Contour Detective lab: add noise, holes, and touching objects, then predict how retrieval modes and area thresholds change the result. What failure case would your robot need to handle first?
