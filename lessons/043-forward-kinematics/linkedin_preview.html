A robot arm cannot reason about “where its gripper is” without a model connecting joint measurements to physical position and orientation.

This Professor OS lesson introduces forward kinematics through a two-link planar RoboRover arm and connects the same idea across diagrams, equations, code, and experiments.

Students learn to:
• resolve each link into horizontal and vertical components;
• distinguish relative joint angles from world-frame directions;
• calculate a complete planar pose;
• verify the mathematics with Python and assertions;
• understand why calibration, backlash, flexibility, and encoder conventions matter on real hardware.

The most valuable habit is to predict before running the program, then compare the predicted pose with the plotted result. Try changing the joint angles and see how each link contributes to the final position.
