Robots do not always need to discover behavior through trial and error. In imitation learning, they can learn from demonstrations supplied by a human or another expert system.

This Professor OS lesson uses RoboRover to make the idea concrete:

• Convert demonstrations into observation-action pairs.
• Build a simple behavior-cloning policy with action counts.
• Compare imitation learning with Q-learning.
• See how distribution shift and compounding error emerge after a small mistake.
• Run a complete Python simulation with unseen states and safety-oriented fallback discussion.

The most important engineering lesson is that copying expert actions does not automatically copy the expert’s goals, reasoning, or safety judgment. Try the lab, remove part of a demonstration, and observe what the learned policy cannot infer.
