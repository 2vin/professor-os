A robot does not simply receive a command and move. It measures what happened, compares that result with a goal, and chooses what to do next.

In this Professor OS lesson, students:

• Trace the Sense → Think → Act loop through a mobile rover
• Distinguish sensors, controllers, actuators, feedback, autonomy, and preprogrammed behavior
• Calculate signed distance error with units
• See how a deadband reduces unnecessary switching
• Run and modify a noisy Python simulation

The lesson also introduces real engineering complications—noise, delay, calibration, saturation, wheel slip, and mechanical limits—without losing the beginner-friendly core idea.

Try the lab, then change the noise level, deadband, or speed and observe how the rover’s behavior changes. What would your controller do if the sensor failed?
