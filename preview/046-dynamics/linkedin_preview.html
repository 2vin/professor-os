A payload does more than add weight to a robot. It changes the force required to accelerate, brake, and safely follow a trajectory.

This Professor OS lesson uses RoboRover carrying a toolbox to connect:

• external and internal forces through a clear system boundary;
• net force, mass, and acceleration using Newton’s second law;
• payload effects on velocity, position, traction, and stopping behavior;
• analytic motion equations with semi-implicit Euler simulation;
• prediction, code modification, verification, and a cautious braking extension.

The strongest teaching move is the comparison between an empty and loaded rover under the same net force: students can see why the heavier robot accelerates less, then test that prediction in Python.

Try the payload challenge with learners: change the mass or force, predict the final velocity and position, then compare the result with the simulation. Keep physical testing supervised, bounded, and protected by a verified emergency stop.
