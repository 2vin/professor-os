What makes an autonomous drone more than a flying camera?

This lesson follows the complete reasoning chain from physical motion to software behavior:

• Six degrees of freedom describe the vehicle’s possible motion, not its number of motors.
• Body and world frames explain why tilting redirects thrust and changes altitude behavior.
• Sensor noise, bias, delay, and actuator saturation turn ideal equations into engineering problems.
• A Python altitude-control lab lets students test gain, damping, noise, target changes, and saturation.
• Layered control connects task-level autonomy with state estimation, attitude control, and motor commands.

The strongest learning opportunity is to predict the graph before running the simulation, then explain why force changes acceleration before velocity and altitude change. Try the lab in simulation first, especially before discussing any physical drone test.
